The Human Mind

Whole-Brain Resting Network · Front to Back

The human mind is an ocean of eighty-six billion brain cells constantly talking to one another. Every memory you keep, every sudden hunch, and every emotion you feel begins as tiny electrical signals traveling from cell to cell.

On the right, you are looking at a live 3D electrical-signals view of the human mind, traced from a real MRI scan. You can drag anywhere on the brain to rotate it 360°, hover over different areas to see which emotion lives there, and click any glowing region to explore it.

Along the bottom are core human emotions — Empathy, Wonder, Joy, Vulnerability, Memory, Imagination, and Grief. Click any emotion below (or on the brain) to light up its pathway, see the unique microscopic brain cell inside that region, and read how that feeling shows up in your eyes, lips, breath, and hands.

01 · The Feeling Mind

Microexpressions & Neuroscience

I have always been fascinated by the tiny microexpressions in people — how a slight change in someone's eyes, a pause in their voice, or a shift in their breath can tell you what they are really going through inside. Paying attention to those quiet human signals is what made me fall in love with psychology, neuroscience, and understanding how the human mind works, and it is why I built the 3D brain visualization above.

02 · Learning with AI

AI-Driven Hyper-Growth Learning

Growing up, I wasn't always the best student in traditional classrooms because every mind learns in its own way. When AI came along, it completely changed my life — it helped me learn, ask questions, and connect complex ideas so much faster than I ever could before. Experiencing that firsthand showed me what a massive opportunity AI is for humanity, and I am here to work hard and explore how empathetic AI can unlock hyper-growth learning for everyone.
