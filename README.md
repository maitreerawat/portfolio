## 🌟 The Mission

I am driven by a deep curiosity to **understand human beings** — our behavioral patterns, emotional landscapes, cognitive truths, and lived vulnerabilities.

My purpose is to bridge **Psychology and Artificial Intelligence with radical empathy**, designing thoughtful solutions and assistive systems that solve real problems, heal fractures, and progress humanity forward.

---

## 🧠 Core Pillars

1. **Human Behavior & Cognition:**
   * Decoding why humans feel, react, connect, and struggle.
   * Studying behavioral patterns, cognitive models, and psychological dynamics.

2. **Radical Empathy in Technology:**
   * Placing human dignity, care, and emotional well-being at the heart of machine intelligence.
   * Ensuring technology never alienates, exploits, or dehumanizes who we are.

3. **AI for Human Progress:**
   * Engineering purposeful AI tools and assistive systems that augment human agency.
   * Tackling real-world challenges in mental well-being, learning, and collective flourishing.

4. **The Living Ideas Laboratory:**
   * A vibrant, curious archive of active prototypes, research essays, speculative questions, and thought experiments.

---

## 🛠️ Stack & Architecture

* **Frontend:** Semantic HTML5, [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) for fluid modern typography and styling.
* **Interactions & Motion:** [GSAP (GreenSock)](https://gsap.com/) for physics-based spring interactions, ambient lighting, and smooth page dynamics.
* **Performance:** Zero-build static web architecture for 100/100 Lighthouse performance and zero maintenance overhead.
* **Deployment:** Hosted directly on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/).
